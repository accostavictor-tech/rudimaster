About RudiMaster

RudiMaster started from a simple problem: the metronome lives in one app, the rudiment sheet in another, and practice gets lost between the two. Here both sit in the same place, work on a phone and ask for no login. The audio clock that fires the click is the same one that moves the notation, so sound and image never drift apart.
