Why support it and where the money goes

  • Keeping the tool open and free for anyone practising, with no sign-up and no payment required.
  • Domain and hosting, the fixed costs of keeping the site online.
  • Development time: more rudiments, saved practice routines and tempo history.
  • Every contribution extends how long the project can run without leaning on another source of revenue.

Ways to help that cost nothing

  • Send the link to your teacher, your band or your drum group.
  • Tell me what is missing. Feedback from people who actually practise is what shapes what comes next.
  • If you spot a mistake in a rudiment, say so. Wrong notation gets in the way of practice.

Thank you for keeping this alive.
